Le Domaine aux Lions

Luxury property in the heart of Normandy, just 1 hour 45 minutes from Paris and 20 minutes from Deauville and Honfleur.

We have designed our home with your comfort in mind: the comfort and luxury of a hotel, the facilities of a holiday resort, with added privacy and tranquillity.

Le Domaine aux Lions is located in a small, peaceful hamlet in the heart of the Normandy countryside, surrounded by fields.

Our large, charming and fully renovated house features 8 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, an indoor jacuzzi, a spacious living room with fireplace, pool table (billiards), table football, piano and more.

Outside, enjoy a covered and secure swimming pool, heated to 28°C all year round (temperature may vary during exceptionally cold weather), a terrace with BBQ and table tennis, as well as a tennis court.

WiFi and high-speed fibre internet are available throughout the property.

You will be welcomed in person and everything will be explained to you upon your arrival️
