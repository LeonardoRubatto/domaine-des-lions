Professionals

Le Domaine aux Lions is also available for professional hire. 8 rooms and their annexes can accommodate comfortably up to 15 persons. Inside, a large space that can be organised according to your needs, several meeting tables, a board, smaller separate spaces. Outside, 2 large tables on the terrace and lots of space to work quietly or to do group activities.

For your work seminars, team-building sessions, workshops:

  • white board and markers
  • projector
  • high-speed internet via optic fibre
  • coffee capsules


For your retreats, trainings, and other activities:

  • large reorganisable spaces
  • zero noise or other disturbance

Additional services can be organised on request: catering, private driver, etc.
