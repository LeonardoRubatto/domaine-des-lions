Your stay, in practice.
Your group
The house welcomes up to 15 guests in 8 bedrooms and their annexes. Contact the owners to arrange sleeping configurations for your group.
Length of stay
The estate’s website states a minimum of 2 nights outside school holidays and 5 nights during school holidays. Ask the owners about weekday stays.
Your arrival
Beds are made, and bathroom and pool towels are provided. Cleaning takes place after departure; guests clean the kitchen and dishes, and sort and leave rubbish in the bins behind the house. A €1,500 security deposit is requested.
The pool
The covered, secured pool is heated to 28°C all year. Water temperature can vary during exceptionally cold weather. The stated opening hours are 8am to 8pm.
Peace and quiet
The estate values peace and quiet. No parties or outdoor music, and no noise after 11pm, as stated in the original website’s conditions.
Services on request
Babysitting, activities for children and adults, catering, massages and introductions to suppliers may be available on request. The owners will confirm options and prices.
